ORIGINAL AI MIX
Statement of Principles and Usage Recommendations
ORIGINAL AI MIX is an identification symbol created with the purpose of promoting transparency in the use of Artificial Intelligence in music creation, while respecting artistic freedom, digital ethics, and the legal principles applicable to the online environment.
This symbol does not constitute a quality seal, artistic certification, or technical evaluation. Its function is exclusively informative, allowing the public to recognize when a musical work has been created, in whole or in part, using Artificial Intelligence technologies.
1. Principle of Transparency
The use of the ORIGINAL AI MIX logo is based on the principle of transparency, acknowledging the public’s right to clear information regarding the creative processes involved in music production.
2. Creative Freedom
ORIGINAL AI MIX does not impose creative restrictions nor value judgments on the use of Artificial Intelligence. AI is understood as a legitimate artistic creation tool, capable of coexisting with human contributions at different levels.
3. Identification, Not Certification
The ORIGINAL AI MIX symbol is intended exclusively for the identification of musical content involving AI.
It does not represent:
A guarantee of artistic quality
Technical certification
Institutional approval
Exclusivity of authorship
4. Responsible Use of the Logo
The logo should only be used when there is actual involvement of Artificial Intelligence in the musical creative process, and the user assumes full responsibility for the accuracy of that information.
The logo must be applied while respecting its visual integrity, without graphic alterations, distortions, or unauthorized adaptations, and must always include the credit “Original AI Mix.”
5. Digital Compatibility
ORIGINAL AI MIX is designed to be compatible with:
Album and single covers
Streaming platforms
Social media and promotional materials
Digital metadata and textual credits
Whenever visual use is not possible, an equivalent textual identification is recommended.
6. Protection and Good Faith
The use of ORIGINAL AI MIX must be carried out in good faith and must not be used in a misleading, abusive manner, or in content that does not involve AI-based musical creation.
Any improper use may compromise the right to use the symbol, without prejudice to other applicable legal measures.
7. Evolution and Updates
This text may be updated to accompany technological, legal, and cultural developments in Artificial Intelligence applied to music, while remaining faithful to the principles of transparency, responsibility, and creative freedom.
